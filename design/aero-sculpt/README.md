The 1.25× chassis scale brings the pilot up to scout height. The swept shoulder caps sit on separate lower skirts. Tapered forearm and shin guards leave the hinges exposed, and the split toe shoes extend forward around the original toe hinge. The darker field finish separates the armor from the red identification marks. The R10 source and the revised export recipe reproduce the shipped GLB byte for byte.

The Aero simulation passed.

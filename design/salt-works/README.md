# Salt Works verification

- The simulation gate passed 73 suites, including eight published career saves and 120 seeded maps across five contracts.
- The Salt Works browser scenario passed on desktop and touch. It bought and assigned Saltjack, accepted the Vanta offer, restored both model-backed actors after reload, and used Brace in the separate trial without writing to the career.
- The desktop motion gate measured both actors' live grounded walk and checked the eight launches and eight impacts of the stock Kestrel burst alongside Brace, recoil and the settled wreck.
- The shared runtime GLB is 1,430,140 bytes. Its 12 images are embedded. Both cassettes are parented to the corresponding shoulder joints and only the garrison displays them.

The original release mounted the same gun permanently on both frames. The regression checks now cover the stock Kestrel on both actors, the 180-reputation MG refit, empty grip after removal, per-copy inventory migration and the Vanta salvage threshold.

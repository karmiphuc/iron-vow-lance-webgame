# Nightjar

Blind Crossing at Brine Causeway fields one hostile NJ–07. The left shoulder carries an integral Quill S-7. The right-hand Bulwark buckler redirects hits to that arm with the stock 20% reduction. The hull and gun are unavailable as player equipment.

[Inspect the three stances](stance.webp) · [View the live duel camera](field.webp)

The forelimbs carry the forward hull on narrow knuckles. They fold aft for travel and replant in succession to brace the shot. Each hind leg has a short hip rocker, two folding links, a long lower segment and a small recurved claw. The direction and reach of the lower link follow the planted toe. The shoulder cradle holds its own firing line through the stride. The run and firing arrest draw on the sequences around 46 and 51 seconds in [Mech “Gibbon” Mood & Animation Test](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3VwPPLlG3Ig&t=46s).

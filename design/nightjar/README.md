# Nightjar

Blind Crossing at Brine Causeway fields one hostile NJ–07. The integral Quill S-7 runs on a retracting carriage on the left side of the cockpit. The right-hand Bulwark buckler redirects hits to that arm with the stock 20% reduction. The hull and gun are unavailable as player equipment.

[Inspect the guard, gun hand, tripod and run](stance.webp) · [View the live duel camera](field.webp)

The right hand carries a broad notched shield in front of the torso. As the hull settles, the claw beneath that shield touches down ahead of the two rear feet. The left hand closes around the rifle grip while its carriage moves inboard. The barrel clears the cutout in the shield. For travel, the carriage retracts and both forelimbs fold back into the hind-leg run. The run and firing arrest draw on the sequences around 46 and 51 seconds in [Mech “Gibbon” Mood & Animation Test](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3VwPPLlG3Ig&t=46s).

## Verification

- The browser deployed the Brine contract, reloaded the sortie, and recorded a move, shot, cover step, buckler hit and dodge from the game rig. Sol 6.2 Medium reviewed the running profile, frontal coverage, three-point brace, left-hand grip and live muzzle flash.
- The simulation resolves 27 HP through five armor for 4 AP. A 45-damage incoming shot removes 36 right-arm HP. The shot that breaks the buckler stays on that arm. The Quill continues firing until its own mount breaks.
- The enemy-turn fixture opens the distance from three tiles to seven and spends its AP on two shots. The surrender fixture keeps the frame and weapon out of salvage and inventory.
- The rig gate checks the third claw on flat and sloping ground, frontal shield interception, clearance beside the receiver, the left-hand grip and the muzzle ray. A 240-frame comparison with the approved run put the largest difference across all eight hind-leg joints below 0.000002 game units.

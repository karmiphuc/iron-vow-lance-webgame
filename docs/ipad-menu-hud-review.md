# Iron Vow iPad menus and combat HUD review

## First review slice

Show Company, Bridge, Hiring Desk and one mixed-hardpoint refit side by side with the existing versions. These four screens cover screen density, discoverability, navigation and decision accuracy. Keep the three global departments.

| Priority and surface | Evidence | Proposed change and review check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 · Company | The live roster measured 404 × 275 CSS px per portrait at 1363 × 936. The three images occupy about 26% of that viewport. The previous 1024 × 1366 project capture already fits all three training actions. | Try 64–72 px portraits in rows. Preserve name, callsign, chassis, XP and available training points. Tap a pilot for their larger portrait. At 1024 × 768 show all three starters and their Train actions without scrolling. Include wounded and reserve pilots in the longer-roster test. |
| P1 · Bridge | Train Upgrades has a 44 px-high target with muted 11 px text and transparent border/background. It sits under the selected railhead’s Contracts and Services and opens a screen titled Ship upgrades. | Give Train Upgrades an outlined action beside the chart heading, with an idle or in-progress engineering status. Keep port actions attached to the selected railhead. Test finding upgrades after selecting a distant destination and after returning home. Preserve existing access rules. |
| P1 · Hiring and duty | Duty Roster sends the player to generic Hangar Facilities and focuses Mech Bay. The existing crew-cabin route expands crew but targets the cabin room. | Enter the expanded roster directly, focus its heading, and restore the hiring list on Back. Test with full seats, reserves, wounds and a locked cabin. |
| P1 · Refit accuracy | The comparison takes the first equipped item in the selected weapon class. Strike can contain both right-hand and shoulder equipment. The preview can therefore use a different mount from the one being changed. | Resolve the intended mount using the same loadout logic as Equip. Name the mount, its current item and the damage, AP and range changes. Test a Strike rifle plus a mounted launcher, an empty mount and a legacy loadout. |
| P2 · Refit browsing | Out-of-stock equipment is visible alongside available purchases. The list initially prefers the previous selection, equipped item or first item. | Within each compatible class, show equipped, owned spares and purchasable local stock first. Keep locked stock discoverable and preserve deliberate selection when returning. |
| P2 · Combat feedback | The recent log disappears at widths up to 1100 px. The battle Journal opens Quick Orders and Pilot Wiki. The battle stores only four log lines, and the HUD normally shows two. | Give the latest completed exchange a stable line and a 44 px control to reopen recent exchanges. Attribute attacker, target, result and return fire correctly. Define the retained history before choosing its label. |
| P2 · Combat space | At 1100 px and below the unit and action rows stack. Existing tablet captures feature small deployments. Occlusion in a fully equipped lance still needs reproduction. | Prototype compact ally cards with AP, readiness and critical warnings visible. Show selected-unit details on demand. Measure the map area and action positions with a full lance, auxiliary when available, shield, specials and targeting open. Preserve the attack-camera framing. |
| P2 · Train and bay projects | The queue and lance readiness precede project selection. Collapsed rows foreground tier and price. | Collapse readiness, show each next perk and progress beside its cost, and keep independent train and hangar queues distinguishable. |
| P3 · Company depth | Level-one training has two spendable skills and four locked foundation nodes. A new pilot can have a computer-slot chooser containing only Empty. | Lead with spendable skills and the next unlock. Keep the full doctrine tree and future build planning one tap away. |
| P3 · Contracts, Journal and Career | Contracts already exposes one selected offer and paid scouting. Attack-camera preferences sit with career import, export and reset. | Preserve contract selection across scouting and cancellation. Place known threats, rewards and deploy readiness together. Group audiovisual preferences and keep backups under Manage career. |
| P3 · Debrief | Captures, training points and completed projects can add several follow-up buttons to the same result. | Show payment, damage and captures first. Give captured hulls or a damaged lance a direct follow-up, with remaining rewards and projects linked from their rows. |
| P1 · Contracts return | Back to Bridge invokes global navigation, which defaults to the current railhead. Reviewing a remote contract can discard the selected destination and route. | Return to the selected railhead, chosen route and chart camera, with focus on Contracts. Test after panning to a remote railhead and choosing a different route. |
| P1 · Special-attack preview | Giza Double Cannon and Vyzov Cross Mortar place the tactical preview and minimap in the same lower-left area at tablet widths. Their CSS anchors predict an overlap. A live reproduction is pending. | Test both weapons before and after aiming. Anchor the preview above the action tray with space reserved for the minimap. Assert that the preview, minimap and tray rectangles do not intersect and every control remains tappable. |

## Tablet acceptance

Test 1024 × 768, 1180 × 820 and 820 × 1180 with touch input. Retain 44 × 44 CSS px targets for frequent controls. Check both a fresh career and a progressed save. Verify selection, scroll and focus after Back. Check target inspection, return fire, surrender and debrief in a populated battle. Capture the specific states at those dimensions, then check the main tasks in iPad Safari.

Leg IK · kit bug preview

VowChassis.solveLeg sets the hip with Euler order XYZ, so planted feet miss their IK target by 5–9 cm at rest and up to ~0.6 m on quad legs. The preview flips the hip order to ZXY on the built bones. Not in the game.

Legs own pelvis, hips, gait and leg HP. Core owns torso, head, back units and body HP. Arms own pauldrons, arms, hands and arm HP. Stats for mixed builds are bench-only stand-ins; the game has none yet.

Rig Spec

What this bench is

The mechs come from chassis.js unchanged (VowChassis). All motion comes from a verbatim extract of the game's vow3d.js: animateMech (gait, leg IK, aim, recoil, death poses), the melee timeline, fireFX, meleeUpdate, onStep and the FX pools. Bench edits to that extract are tagged in the source and only swap game globals for a stub.

Joint hierarchy · biped (Warden, Basilisk, Valkyrie, Oni)

container          yaw · death topple · ×0.9 in battle
└─ root
   └─ pelvis       bob · sway · compression spring · legs-dead drop
      ├─ spine     yaw = aim + melee twist · pitch spring · recoil
      │  ├─ neck   aim elevation · idle look
      │  ├─ pauldron.L / .R   follow shoulder x×0.28, z×0.4
      │  ├─ shoulder.R → elbow.R → wrist.R → socket.R  [WEAPON]
      │  └─ shoulder.L → elbow.L → wrist.L → socket.L  [empty]
      ├─ hip.L → knee.L → ankle.L   2-bone IK · knee forward
      └─ hip.R → knee.R → ankle.R   toes out 0.17 rad

Reverse-joint (Kestrel legs)

pelvis ─ hip → knee → ankle   same bones; solveLeg flips the bend:
                              hip.x = −(φ − α), knee.x = −bend
                              knee sits behind the hip–ankle line

Quad (Behemoth legs)

pelvis
├─ mount.LF  yaw atan2(+x,+z) → hip → knee → ankle
├─ mount.RF  yaw atan2(−x,+z) → hip → knee → ankle
├─ mount.LB  yaw atan2(+x,−z) → hip → knee → ankle
└─ mount.RB  yaw atan2(−x,−z) → hip → knee → ankle

Damage-part contract

parts: body pilot right left legs  → [{m, detach, np}]
state = HP 0 → 2 destroyed · HP < 50% → 1 damaged · else 0
0 intact     base material
1 damaged    damagedOf(): scorched paint
2 destroyed  detach meshes hidden + debris; right → arm hangs (weapon offline);
             legs → pelvis drops, immobile; pilot → kneel, lights off; body → topple
wreck        wreckOf() on paint materials, visor and stacks dark

Weight model (game values)

Gait phase advances at speed ÷ (2 × step length); step length 1.7 m, speed = 3.4 m ÷ the legs' MECH_MOTION step (0.50–0.78 s). Feet stay planted in world space; the swing foot follows a fast-lift, slow-set arc with toe pitch. Footfalls kick pelvis compression (80/11), spine pitch (55/7), arm dip (60/7) and camera shake, scaled by HEAVY. Melee: wind-up 0–0.38 s, strike to 0.5 s, contact 0.46 s, hitstop 0.085 s, recover by 1.1 s, lunge up to 2.6 m toward the target.

War mode rules (bench-only)

Fixed 60 Hz tick, mulberry32 PRNG seeded from the seed box for every sim decision (draft, cover, targets, part rolls, hits, detours). The draft is stratified: each battle fields all six cores and every weapon twice, and any three consecutive soak battles cover all 30 core × weapon and all 30 legs × weapon cells. Leaving War frees the arena; coming back restarts the last seed from tick 0. Hit parts: body 34%, pilot 10% (−27% to hit, half damage), weapon arm 17%, left arm 17%, legs 22%. A round on a destroyed arm or leg passes into the body. Weapon arm destroyed: weapon offline, unit falls back. Left arm destroyed: −15% accuracy. Legs under 50%: 60% speed; destroyed: immobile. Pilot or body at zero: out. A side whose live units all have their weapon arm destroyed loses (disarmed); otherwise the battle times out at 180 s. Cover between shooter and target: −40% to hit (missiles ignore cover).

Anomaly checks (every tick)

  • nan · any bone position, quaternion or scale not finite
  • footGround · planted ankle more than 0.15 m above or below its ground height
  • footSlide · planted ankle drifts more than 0.3 m from where it planted
  • ikReach · foot target more than 0.03 m beyond thigh + shin
  • kneeDir · knee more than 0.054 m on the wrong side of the hip–ankle line for its leg type
  • bounds · unit centre outside the arena
  • overlap · two live footprints closer than 80% of their radii, or a unit inside a cover block
  • stuck · wants to move, moved under 1 m in 4 s
  • weaponMount · weapon mount not parented to socket.R
  • muzzleFar · muzzle 0.9 m further from the hand than at spawn
  • noDamage · a landed round or blade contact on a live target dealt ≤ 0
  • deadPartDamage · damage applied to a part already at 0 HP
  • hpRange · HP below 0, above max, or not finite
  • wreckActs · a downed unit fires, has velocity or moves
  • partVisual · part state on the model ≠ state implied by HP
  • triJump · a unit's draw calls or triangles grow after damage, or GPU geometry count grows by 60+
  • frameSpike · a frame (live) or tick (soak) slower than 50 ms and 5× the running average

What the bench cannot reproduce

  • The game moves tile by tile in turns; War mode moves continuously in real time. Gait, IK and timelines are the game's, the battle flow is not.
  • Weapon damage, range, accuracy and part HP are stand-ins: the game's weapon table, hit chance and save data are not in this package. Mixed-build stats are undefined in the game.
  • No game save, pilots, XP, workshop, paint shop presets (the game's nine presets live in the game data; the bench offers the kit's twelve palettes), cover rules or 2D fallback.
  • The game's ground texture (concrete.webp) is an embedded game asset; the arena uses a procedural stand-in. No rain or storm lightning.
  • Walk in Bench mode is on a treadmill belt; in the game the mech crosses the board. The legs and feet do exactly what the game does.
  • The v1 bench's six bench-only weapons are not in the kit, so this bench shows the kit's five.
